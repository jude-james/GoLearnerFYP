WHAT IS GOLEARNER?

GoLearner is an online interactive tutorial for Go (Golang), the programming language designed by Google. This tutorial focuses particularly on Go's concurrency, and has some interactive examples you can try out.

WHY USE GOLEARNER?

Concurrency is one of the most powerful and challenging aspects of Go. GoLearner is designed to make it easier to understand through it's interactive lessons and animations. Whether you're completely new to Go or looking to strengthen your understanding of goroutines and channels, GoLearner provides a structured and approachable way to learn.

ABOUT GO

Go is a general purpose high-level programming language designed by Google in 2007. Built around simplicity and efficiency, it combines the ease of use of high-level languages with the speed of low-level languages. Go is statically typed, compiled, garbage-collected, and has built-in CSP concurrency; which is one of it's distinctive features.
